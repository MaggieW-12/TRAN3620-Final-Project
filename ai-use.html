AI use declaration

What the agent did—and what I checked

A concise account of tools, prompts, manual checks, mistakes, and the limits of automation in this project.

On this page Tools used Division of work Prompts AI errors Limits

Tools used

  • OpenAI Codex desktop agent, GPT-5-based. Used to inspect the brief, plan the corpus, write the reproducible Python workflow, design the site, draft interpretation, and debug the result.
  • Local Python. Used to stream the OpenSubtitles files, apply the fixed filters, sample entries, calculate counts, and verify archive line numbers.
  • D3 v7 and d3-sankey. Stored locally for the linked Sankey, heatmap, and force network.
  • Microsoft Word and open-source translation-studies sources. Used as theoretical background, not as corpus data.
Data integrity No subtitle sentence or translation was generated by AI. Every corpus entry is an aligned pair from the downloaded OpenSubtitles archive.

使用工具

  • OpenAI Codex 桌面代理，基於 GPT-5。 用於閱讀作業說明、規劃語料、撰寫可重現的 Python 流程、設計網站、 草擬詮釋與除錯。
  • 本機 Python。 用於串流讀取 OpenSubtitles 檔案、套用固定篩選、抽取樣本、計算數量， 並核對來源行號。
  • D3 v7 與 d3-sankey。 本機儲存，用於互相連動的桑基圖、熱圖與力導向網絡。
  • Microsoft Word 與翻譯研究文獻。 僅作理論背景，不作為語料資料。
資料完整性 AI 沒有生成任何字幕句子或翻譯。每一筆語料都來自下載的 OpenSubtitles 對齊檔案。

What AI did and what I did

Task AI My check / judgement
Corpus retrieval Located and downloaded the official archive; recorded URL, date, size, and SHA-256. Checked the source and licence information; approved the topic filter.
Sampling Implemented the fixed-seed two-stage random sample. Reviewed the method and counts; rejected the first use of an unstructured full scan as inefficient.
Classification Applied the four-rule codebook consistently and wrote notes for each line. Tested the categories on a pilot, changed the cue filter, and inspected awkward cases.
Quality check Reopened the archive and compared 50 randomly selected entries with their exact line numbers. The check found 0 transcription mismatches; semantic and contextual limitations remain.
Website Built search, linked filters, Sankey, heatmap, force network, sortable table, CSV export, and the bilingual interface. Checked charts against table counts and revised labels after visual tests.
Writing Drafted the report, codebook, and AI declaration from the observed data. Reviewed claims against counts; kept limitations explicit and removed overconfident conclusions.

Two prompts that worked well

“Build a reproducible random sample from the OpenSubtitles English-Chinese archive. State the seed, filters, rejected counts, and exact archive line number for every retained pair. Do not generate or paraphrase any corpus text.”

This prompt worked because it defined provenance, sampling, and a non-negotiable prohibition on invented data in one request.

“Check every chart number against the filtered table and report any mismatch before changing the design.”

This shifted the agent from visual production to evidence checking.

兩個有效的提示詞

「從 OpenSubtitles 英漢檔案建立可重現的隨機樣本。說明種子、篩選、 拒絕數量，並為每個保留句對記錄準確的檔案行號。不得生成或改寫任何語料文字。」

這個提示有效，因為它在同一個要求中定義來源、抽樣，以及不可協商的 禁止捏造資料。

「在修改設計前，逐項核對每張圖的數字與篩選後表格是否一致， 並報告任何不符。」

這把代理從視覺製作轉向證據核對。

What AI got wrong

The first humor filter treated as if as a play signal. This pulled in lines such as “It looks as if…” that were ordinary conditionals, not jokes. I noticed because a random sample of the output contained repeated non-humorous phrases. The phrase was removed and the corpus was rebuilt.

An earlier chart plan also grouped source cues and translation strategies as though they were the same kind of field. That would have made the network misleading. The categories were separated: humor cue and strategy now cross in the heatmap and Sankey but are never treated as identical labels.

AI 犯的錯誤

第一個幽默篩選把 as if 當成遊戲訊號，因而納入 「It looks as if…」等普通條件句，而非笑話。我從輸出隨機樣本中看到 重複的非幽默句子後發現問題，移除該詞並重建語料。

早期圖表方案也曾把來源提示與翻譯策略當成同一類欄位， 會讓網絡誤導。後來我把類別分開：幽默提示與策略在熱圖和桑基圖中交叉， 但從不視為相同標籤。

Limits and responsibility

AI can apply a fixed rule quickly and repeatedly. It cannot know whether a viewer laughs, understand a whole film’s comic timing from one line, or take academic responsibility for the chosen theory. The corpus is a screened sample of subtitle dialogue containing explicit cues, not a complete inventory of all Chinese-English jokes.

The final categories, report claims, and public-facing presentation should be reviewed by the student before submission and discussed orally. The 50 checked IDs are available through the explorer’s “Checked entries only” filter.

限制與責任

AI 能快速且反覆套用固定規則，卻不知道觀眾是否真的發笑，也無法只憑 一行字幕理解整部作品的喜劇節奏，更不能替學生承擔理論選擇的學術責任。 本語料是含有明確提示的字幕對話篩選樣本，不是所有英漢笑話的完整清單。

最終類別、報告宣稱與公開呈現，應由學生在提交前審查，並能在口頭報告中 說明。50 個已核對編號可透過探索器的「只顯示已核對項」查看。
