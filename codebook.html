Unit and coding workflow

The coding unit is one aligned subtitle sentence pair. The object of classification is not whether the line is objectively amusing, but how the Chinese translation handles the English humor cue.

First locate the English cue: a joke reference, laughter, an evaluative word such as funny, or a play signal such as ha-ha. Then compare the full Chinese line. Apply the rules in the order below. Stop at the first rule that clearly applies.

  1. Does the Chinese remove or substantially reduce the cue? → compression.
  2. Does it add an explanatory or connective marker plus expansion? → explicitation.
  3. Does it replace the expression while retaining an effect? → functional equivalence.
  4. Otherwise, is the same cue or proposition retained? → direct retention.

A category describes a textual relation, not the translator’s intention or the audience’s real-time laughter. One line may be a poor subtitle and still be coded directly if the wording remains.

標註單位與流程

標註單位是一組對齊的字幕句對。分類對象不是句子客觀上是否好笑， 而是中文譯文如何處理英語幽默提示。

首先找出英語提示：笑話提及、笑聲、funny 等評價詞，或 ha-ha 等遊戲訊號。然後比較完整中文行，依下列順序套用規則， 在第一條明確符合的規則停下。

  1. 中文刪除或實質減弱提示？→ 壓縮。
  2. 中文加入解釋或連接標記並擴充？→ 明晰化。
  3. 中文替換表達但保留效果？→ 功能等值。
  4. 其餘情況，若保留相同提示或命題？→ 直接保留。

類別描述的是文本關係，不是譯者意圖或觀眾當下的笑聲。一行字幕可能 譯得生硬，只要措辭仍保留，仍可標為直接保留。

Category definitions

1. Direct retention

348 / 500
Definition
The Chinese retains the same humor cue, image, or proposition without adding a substantive explanation or replacing it with a different target expression.
Theory source
Direct translation and formal correspondence in Vinay & Darbelnet (1995); semantic equivalence in Baker (2018).
Decision rule
Use only after the compression, explicitation, and substitution rules do not apply, and a comparable cue or proposition remains.
Positive example
EN: Just kidding. The defense rests. ZH: 開個玩笑 辯護結束。 OS24-003750144. The joke cue remains explicit.
Negative example
EN: Don’t be ridiculous. It’s the middle of the night. ZH: 開什麼玩笑 這深更半夜的。 Not direct: the English evaluative cue ridiculous becomes a conventional Chinese challenge.
Boundary case
“She’s a looker. You weren’t kidding.” → 「她很好看，沒開玩笑」 is direct because the kidding cue remains explicit, even though the first clause is reordered.

2. Functional equivalence

65 / 500
Definition
The Chinese changes the wording or cue family but preserves a comparable interactional effect: teasing, disbelief, verbal play, or comic evaluation.
Theory source
Baker’s phrase-level and pragmatic equivalence (2018); Gottlieb’s substitution/paraphrase options in subtitling (1992).
Decision rule
Use when an idiom, conventional challenge, or different cue family replaces the source expression without adding a full explanation.
Positive example
EN: Don’t be ridiculous. It’s the middle of the night. ZH: 開什麼玩笑 這深更半夜的。 OS24-007725657. The conventional challenge carries disbelief.
Negative example
EN: Just kidding. The defense rests. ZH: 開個玩笑 辯護結束。 The joking cue is retained rather than substituted.
Boundary case
If the target both adds a conventional idiom and an explicit explanation, explicitation wins because the hierarchy is checked before functional equivalence.

3. Explicitation / amplification

2 / 500
Definition
The Chinese makes an implied relation explicit, or adds an explanatory connector or emphasizer that expands the English proposition.
Theory source
Amplification in Vinay & Darbelnet (1995); explicitation and pragmatic adjustment in Baker (2018).
Decision rule
Require both an explanatory/connective marker and a visibly expanded Chinese line. A single target particle does not qualify.
Positive example
EN: I don’t mean laughing all the time. I mean, you just don’t look happy. ZH: 我沒有逼你每天都笑，我是說，你看起來一點不開心。 OS24-000602511. The relation is spelled out in Chinese.
Negative example
EN: Don’t be ridiculous. ZH: 開什麼玩笑。 This is a functional idiom substitution unless an additional explanation is present.
Boundary case
A fuller Chinese syntax that remains a normal translation of the same proposition is direct; expansion must add information or a relation rather than merely satisfy Chinese grammar.

4. Compression / neutralization

85 / 500
Definition
The Chinese shortens the line, omits the humor cue, or renders it so flatly that the explicit comic signal is difficult to recover from the target text alone.
Theory source
Condensation in Gottlieb (1992); textual reduction and loss in Delabastita’s discussion of wordplay translation (1996).
Decision rule
Apply first when an explicit source cue is absent from the Chinese cue lexicon and the target is substantially reduced. It is not an error judgment.
Positive example
EN: He can’t, he’s too small Ha Ha He! ZH: 他不行，他太矮了。 OS24-003695288. The laughter signal is absent.
Negative example
EN: That’s funny. You’re a funny guy. ZH: 真好笑，你這個人真有意思。 The target cue remains visible.
Boundary case
“Funny thing…” → 「奇怪的是……」 changes the cue to curiosity or oddity. If the affective signal remains explicit enough to recover, code functional equivalence; if the line is shortened and the cue disappears, code compression.

類別定義

1. 直接保留

348 / 500
定義
中文保留相同的幽默提示、意象或命題，沒有加入實質解釋， 也沒有改換成不同的目標語表達。
理論來源
Vinay & Darbelnet（1995）的直接翻譯與形式對應； Baker（2018）的語意等值。
判定規則
只有在壓縮、明晰化與替代規則都不適用，且仍保留可比提示或 命題時才使用。
正例
EN: Just kidding. The defense rests. ZH: 開個玩笑 辯護結束。 OS24-003750144。開玩笑提示明確保留。
反例
EN: Don’t be ridiculous. It’s the middle of the night. ZH: 開什麼玩笑 這深更半夜的。 不是直接保留：英語評價詞變成漢語慣用質疑。
邊界案例
「She’s a looker. You weren’t kidding.」→ 「她很好看，沒開玩笑」屬直接保留，因為開玩笑提示仍然明確， 即使前一小句次序改變。

2. 功能等值

65 / 500
定義
中文改變措辭或提示類別，但保留可比的互動效果：調侃、不信、 雙關或喜劇性評價。
理論來源
Baker（2018）的短語與語用等值；Gottlieb（1992）的字幕 替代與改寫選項。
判定規則
慣用語、慣常挑戰或不同提示類別取代來源表達，且沒有加入完整 解釋時使用。
正例
EN: Don’t be ridiculous. It’s the middle of the night. ZH: 開什麼玩笑 這深更半夜的。 OS24-007725657。慣用挑戰承載不信。
反例
EN: Just kidding. The defense rests. ZH: 開個玩笑 辯護結束。 玩笑提示被保留，而非替代。
邊界案例
若目標行同時加入慣用語與明確解釋，明晰化優先，因為層級規則 先檢查明晰化。

3. 明晰化／增譯

2 / 500
定義
中文把隱含關係說清楚，或加入解釋性、連接性、強調性標記， 擴充了英語命題。
理論來源
Vinay & Darbelnet（1995）的增譯；Baker（2018）的 明晰化與語用調節。
判定規則
需同時具備解釋／連接標記與可見的中文擴充；單一語氣詞不夠。
正例
EN: I don’t mean laughing all the time. I mean, you just don’t look happy. ZH: 我沒有逼你每天都笑，我是說，你看起來一點不開心。 OS24-000602511。中文把關係說得更明白。
反例
EN: Don’t be ridiculous. ZH: 開什麼玩笑。 沒有額外解釋時，屬慣用語功能替代。
邊界案例
只是為了符合中文語法而使用較完整句法，仍算直接保留；增譯必須 加入資訊或關係，而非只讓句子通順。

4. 壓縮／中和

85 / 500
定義
中文縮短句子、刪除幽默提示，或把提示譯得過於平淡，使單看目標行 難以恢復明確的喜劇訊號。
理論來源
Gottlieb（1992）的壓縮；Delabastita（1996）對雙關翻譯中 文本縮減與損失的討論。
判定規則
當來源有明確提示、詞表中找不到中文提示，且目標行明顯縮短時 優先使用。這不是錯誤判決。
正例
EN: He can’t, he’s too small Ha Ha He! ZH: 他不行，他太矮了。 OS24-003695288。笑聲訊號消失。
反例
EN: That’s funny. You’re a funny guy. ZH: 真好笑，你這個人真有意思。 目標提示仍然可見。
邊界案例
「Funny thing…」→「奇怪的是……」把提示改成好奇或古怪。若情感訊號 仍足可辨識，標功能等值；若句子縮短且提示消失，標壓縮。

Boundary principles

Context beats words Read the full aligned line before coding.
Effect, not intention Code visible textual relations, not guessed motives.
One primary category Use the ordered hierarchy to resolve overlap.
Flag uncertainty Awkward or context-dependent lines stay visible.

邊界原則

語境重於詞語 標註前先讀完整對齊行。
效果而非動機 標註可見文本關係，不猜測譯者動機。
單一主要類別 以層級規則解決重疊。
標出不確定 生硬或依賴語境的行仍保持可見。

Pilot and change log

Version Change Reason
v0.1 Four draft categories tested on 30 entries. Test whether the hierarchy could be applied consistently.
v0.2 Removed as if from the humor lexicon. It admitted many literal conditional phrases that were not humor.
v0.3 Added a target-script threshold. Code-switched or English-dominant lines produced noisy alignments.
v1.0 Locked four categories and their decision order. Categories overlap; an explicit order is more reproducible than separate intuitions.
Known limitation No second independent human annotator was available. The project reports exact line verification for 50 entries, not inter-annotator agreement.
