The joke is only half the translation

A study of 500 English-Chinese subtitle exchanges, from an explicit joke cue to its translated afterlives.

On this page Origin Background Gap Questions Method Analysis Conclusion Reflection References

Origin

I chose jokes because they expose the limit of the reassuring question: “Did the translator keep the words?” A joke may keep every word and still stop being funny.

English-Chinese subtitle dialogue offered a good test case. It is conversational, time-constrained, frequently idiomatic, and much less studied than literary wordplay in English-Chinese translation. The topic also made the corpus interesting to explore: a visitor can begin with joke or 笑, move through a network of cue terms, and end with the subtitle line that motivated the pattern.

The final title, “After the Punchline,” reflects the object of study. I am not trying to decide whether a translation is “correct.” I am asking what remains visible to a Chinese viewer after the English cue has been moved, rewritten, explained, or removed.

緣起

我選擇笑話，因為它暴露了「譯者有沒有保留字面」這個安心問題的限制： 一句笑話可以保留每一個詞，卻不再好笑。

英漢字幕對話適合檢驗這個問題。它口語化、受時間限制、充滿慣用語， 而且比英漢文學雙關研究得更少。這個題目也適合建立可探索的語料： 訪客可以從 joke 或「笑」開始，沿著幽默提示的關係網絡移動， 最後回到造成這項模式的字幕行。

最終標題「笑聲之後」指向研究對象。我無意判定某個譯法是否「正確」， 而是要問：當英語提示被移動、改寫、解釋或刪除之後，中文觀眾仍看得見什麼？

Background

Translation studies often separates direct procedures such as borrowing, calque, and literal translation from oblique procedures such as transposition, modulation, equivalence, and adaptation (Vinay & Darbelnet, 1995). Mona Baker’s work on equivalence is useful here because it shifts attention from isolated words to meaning, idioms, and pragmatic effect across levels of language (Baker, 2018).

Subtitles add a second constraint: space and reading time. Gottlieb describes condensation and substitution among the options available to subtitlers (Gottlieb, 1992). Humor raises the stakes because its effect depends on shared knowledge, timing, and the audience’s expectation of a punchline (Chiaro, 2010; Delabastita, 1996).

This project therefore treats translation strategy as an observable relation between an English humor cue and the Chinese line, not as a judgement about a translator’s talent.

背景

翻譯研究通常區分「直接」程序與「間接」程序：前者包括借用、仿譯、 直譯，後者包括詞性轉換、調節、等值與改編（Vinay & Darbelnet, 1995）。Mona Baker 的等值研究在此很有用，因為它把注意力從孤立詞語 轉向語言各層次的意義、慣用語與語用效果（Baker, 2018）。

字幕還有第二重限制：空間與閱讀時間。Gottlieb 把壓縮與替代列為 字幕譯者可用的方法（Gottlieb, 1992）。幽默更棘手，因為效果依賴 共享知識、節奏，以及觀眾對笑點的期待（Chiaro, 2010; Delabastita, 1996）。

因此，本計畫把翻譯策略視為英語幽默提示與中文行之間可觀察的關係， 而不是對譯者能力的判決。

Gap

Existing English-Chinese humor studies often focus on a small set of literary puns or one translator’s choices. Open subtitle corpora are large, but humor is not a standard metadata field. The gap is therefore practical as well as theoretical: how can a student build a reproducible, inspectable sample of humorous subtitle dialogue and connect each pattern back to a visible cue?

This project does not claim to solve humor detection. It provides a transparent keyword-based screening frame, a theory-linked codebook, and an interface for questioning both.

研究缺口

既有的英漢幽默研究常集中於少量文學雙關，或單一譯者的選擇。 開放字幕語料雖大，卻沒有標準的「幽默」中介資料欄位。因此， 研究缺口既是理論的，也是實作的：學生如何建立可重現、可檢查的 幽默字幕對話樣本，並把每項模式連結回看得見的提示？

本計畫不宣稱解決幽默辨識問題。我提供的是一套透明的關鍵詞篩選框、 與理論連結的編碼手冊，以及一個能讓兩者都受到質疑的介面。

Questions

  1. Which translation strategies dominate a sample of English-Chinese subtitle exchanges with explicit humor cues?
  2. Does the probability of reworking the line change by cue type, such as joke/kidding, laughter, or funny/absurd?
  3. What can linked cue-strategy views reveal that a table of 500 lines cannot show on its own?

研究問題

  1. 在帶有明確幽默提示的英漢字幕樣本中，哪些翻譯策略最常見？
  2. 改寫句子的可能性是否因提示類型而改變，例如「笑話／開玩笑」、 「笑聲」或「好笑／荒謬」？
  3. 互相連結的提示與策略視圖，能揭示五百行表格本身看不見的什麼？

Method

22,394,812 aligned source pairs
300,000 random screening pairs
878 qualifying humor candidates
500 final random sample

I started from the OpenSubtitles v2024 English-Simplified Chinese Moses archive distributed by OPUS, retrieved on 10 November 2026. The archive SHA-256 is recorded in the sampling metadata. Using seed 3620, I drew 300,000 distinct aligned line numbers uniformly from all 22,394,812 pairs. This screening sample keeps the procedure reproducible without scanning only the opening block of a massive subtitle collection.

A pair had to contain non-empty text, Latin letters in English and CJK characters in Chinese, 5–32 English words, 3–100 Chinese CJK characters, and a match to a fixed humor-cue lexicon. I removed exact duplicate source lines and excluded targets where Latin text made up an excessive share of the line. The 878 qualifying candidates were ranked by a second fixed hash, and the lowest 500 hashes form the corpus. This prevents “first 500” or “favourite 500” selection.

Before full coding, I tested four draft categories on 30 entries. The pilot exposed two problems: as if generated many non-humorous conditional phrases, and code-switched or English-dominant targets produced noisy alignments. I removed as if from the humor cues, added a target-script threshold, and retained the codebook rules as a hierarchy. The full change log appears on the codebook page.

I randomly sampled 50 of the final entries and checked both lines against the exact archive line number. All 50 texts matched the source, with zero transcription mismatches; those entries are marked human_checked=yes. This check establishes traceability, not semantic perfection. Some volunteer subtitle alignments remain context-dependent or awkward, which is reported as a limitation rather than hidden.

我以 OPUS 發佈的 OpenSubtitles v2024 英漢摩西格式檔案為起點， 擷取日期為 2026 年 11 月 10 日；原始檔 SHA-256 記錄於 取樣中介資料。 我以種子 3620，從全部 22,394,812 組句對均勻抽取 300,000 個不同的 對齊行號。這個初篩樣本讓程序可重現，也避免只掃描龐大語料的開頭區塊。

句對必須兩側非空、英語含拉丁字母、中文含 CJK 字元、英語為 5–32 詞、 中文為 3–100 個 CJK 字元，並符合固定的幽默提示詞表。我移除完全重複的 英語來源行，也排除拉丁文字比例過高的目標行。878 個合格候選再依第二個 固定雜湊值排序，取最低的 500 筆。這避免「前 500 筆」或「最喜歡的 500 筆」 式選樣。

全面編碼前，我先以 30 筆測試四個草擬類別。初測暴露兩個問題： as if 會產生大量非幽默條件句；語碼混用或英語為主的目標行 會造成雜訊對齊。我從幽默提示中移除 as if，加入目標語言文字 比例門檻，並保留編碼手冊的層級規則。完整修訂紀錄見 編碼手冊。

我從最終語料隨機抽取 50 筆，依原始檔的準確行號核對兩側文本。 50 筆全部與來源相符，沒有轉錄錯誤；這些項目標記為 human_checked=yes。這項核對證明可追溯性，不代表語意完美。 部分志願者字幕對齊仍依賴語境或略顯生硬；報告把這點列為限制，而非隱藏。

Analysis

The headline finding is conservative translation: direct retention is the largest category. But the aggregate hides a sharper difference by cue type.

Strategy distribution
n = 500

“From cue to strategy”

The Sankey view shows that 348 of 500 entries (69.6%) retain the humor cue without a major explanatory addition or substitution. Compression or neutralization is second at 85 (17.0%), followed by functional equivalence at 65 (13.0%). Explicitation is rare: only two entries (0.4%). This suggests that in this screened subtitle frame, literal or near-literal transfer is common, but it is not uniform across the cue types.

“Where the pressure falls”

The heatmap makes the cue-level contrast visible. Of 208 funny/absurd entries, 174 (83.7%) are direct. Of 122 laughter entries, 97 (79.5%) are direct. The pattern changes for explicit joke signals: only 65 of 136 joke/kidding entries (47.8%) are direct, while 50 (36.8%) use functional equivalence. Play signals are most likely to be compressed or neutralized (14 of 34, 41.2%). A likely explanation is that “funny” and laughter can remain as evaluative cues, while an explicit joke often requires the translator to rebuild the interaction rather than merely carry a word.

“The translation network”

The force network maps English cue categories to Chinese cue families. It is an exploratory linkage view, not a complete map of omission. In 204 of 500 entries the target has no explicit Chinese cue in my lexicon, so those lines are absent from the network and remain visible in the heatmap as compression/neutralization or direct rendering. The network is most useful for tracing recurring correspondences, such as joke-family terms connecting to both direct and functionally adapted Chinese expressions.

“Every line has a trail”

The linked data grid is the accountability layer. Every chart click becomes a visible subset, and the table exposes the sentence pair, category, check status, source line number, and classification note. This matters because a persuasive chart can still conceal a weak example. The table makes that risk inspectable.

分析

最重要的發現是「保守翻譯」：直接保留是最大類別。但整體數字掩蓋了 不同提示類型之間的鮮明差異。

翻譯策略分布
n = 500

「從提示到策略」

桑基圖顯示，五百筆中有 348 筆（69.6%）在沒有大幅增譯或替代的情況下 保留幽默提示。壓縮／中和居次，共 85 筆（17.0%）；功能等值為 65 筆 （13.0%）；明晰化極少，只有 2 筆（0.4%）。這表示在此次篩選的字幕 區塊中，直譯或近直譯相當常見，但並非各類提示都相同。

「張力落在哪裡」

熱圖呈現提示層面的差異。208 筆「好笑／荒謬」中，174 筆（83.7%） 為直接保留；122 筆笑聲中，97 筆（79.5%）為直接保留。明確笑話訊號 則不同：136 筆「笑話／開玩笑」中，只有 65 筆（47.8%）直接保留， 另有 50 筆（36.8%）使用功能等值。遊戲訊號最可能被壓縮或中和 （34 筆中的 14 筆，41.2%）。一個合理解釋是：「好笑」與笑聲可以 作為評價性提示直接保留，而明確笑話往往要求譯者重建互動，而非只移動詞語。

「翻譯關係網絡」

力導向網絡把英語提示類別連結到漢語提示類別。這是探索性的關係視圖， 不是完整的省略地圖。五百筆中有 204 筆的目標行不含詞表所辨識的明確 漢語提示，因此不顯示於網絡，但仍在熱圖中呈現為壓縮／中和或直接保留。 網絡最適合追蹤反覆出現的對應，例如笑話類詞語如何同時連向直接與 功能改寫的中文表達。

「每一行都有來源線索」

互相連動的資料表是問責層。每次點擊圖表都會變成可見的子集，表格則 展示句對、類別、核對狀態、來源行號與分類說明。這很重要，因為有說服力 的圖表仍可能掩蓋薄弱例子；表格讓這種風險可被檢查。

Conclusion

In these 500 subtitle exchanges, humor survives most visibly as direct wording, but explicit joke cues create more pressure to change the expression. The strongest result is not a universal law of translation. It is a discipline of comparison: search the cue, compare the line, classify the relation, and inspect the source.

The project achieves three things: a reproducible 500-entry corpus, an interactive explorer in which every visual responds to the same state, and a documented interpretation that distinguishes evidence from conjecture.

結論

在這五百組字幕對話中，幽默最常以直接措辭留下；但明確的笑話提示 帶來更大的改寫壓力。最強的結果不是一條普遍翻譯定律，而是一種比較紀律： 搜尋提示、比較原譯行、分類關係，再檢查來源。

本計畫完成三件事：可重現的五百筆語料；所有視圖都回應同一篩選狀態的 互動探索器；以及把證據與推測分開的公開詮釋。

Reflection

The hardest part was not downloading data or drawing charts. It was keeping a large corpus from looking more certain than it is. Humor depends on context that a single aligned line often removes. Keyword screening creates recall and precision problems at the same time: it misses jokes without cue words and admits phrases that mention humor without performing it.

If I began again, I would pilot two independent human annotators on 30 entries before automation and calculate agreement. I would also build a context window of the surrounding subtitle lines, because “funny” in one line may only be understandable in the next. In this version, I chose a transparent limitation over a false claim of complete semantic coding.

AI was useful for streaming the archive, applying filters consistently, building the site, and producing testable summaries. It could not decide which line a human viewer actually finds funny, and it could not take responsibility for the final categories. Those remain interpretive decisions that need human review.

反思

最難的部分不是下載資料或畫圖，而是避免讓龐大語料看起來比實際更確定。 幽默依賴語境，而單一對齊行往往移除語境。關鍵詞篩選同時產生召回率與 精確率問題：它會漏掉沒有提示詞的笑話，也會納入只是提及幽默、卻沒有 實際產生幽默效果的句子。

如果重新開始，我會在自動化前請兩位獨立人工標註者先測試約 30 筆， 並計算一致性。我也會建立包含前後字幕行的語境視窗，因為某行的 「好笑」可能要到下一行才成立。在這個版本中，我選擇公開限制， 而不是虛稱已完成完整的語意編碼。

AI 對串流讀取檔案、一致套用篩選、建立網站與產出可測試摘要很有用。 但它不能決定人類觀眾實際覺得哪一行好笑，也不能替最終類別負責。 這些仍是需要人工審查的詮釋判斷。
